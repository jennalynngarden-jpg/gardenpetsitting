Services

Every pet and home is different, so every experience is tailored. Georgia has cared for dogs, cats, birds, hamsters, guinea pigs, and fish.

In-home visits

Georgia visits your home 1-2 times a day while you’re away. Your pets keep their routine and their favorite napping spots and you get photo updates with every visit.

  • Walks and play with ball, toys, or frisbee
  • Feeding at their usual times, with special diets followed
  • Medication right on schedule: pills, drops, and treatments
  • Pet areas kept tidy: bowls, litter, potty pickup, and swept floors

Dog daycare

At Georgia’s home in South Salinas

Have your pup spend the day at the Garden family home with a big backyard, plenty of toys, and company from drop-off to pickup.

  • A large fenced backyard with toys and sunny sleeping spots
  • Walks, play, company, and attention all day from Georgia and family
  • Every new dog starts with a free meet and greet with Luna, Georgia’s dog
  • Only available for dogs who are non-reactive around other dogs, cats, and kids
